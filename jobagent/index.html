Job Agent

Automating repetitive work is what I do for a living. It would be strange to run my own job search by hand.

Why I built it

A job search is mostly the kind of work I automate for others: checking the same boards every day, sorting mail, typing the same details into one form after another. So I gave that part to an agent.

The time it saves goes into the part a machine shouldn’t do: picking the companies I actually want, fixing drafts that don’t sound like me, and getting ready for the calls. I set the rules for which roles it goes after and who it may write to, every portal application waits for my yes, and your reply comes straight to me.

If you’re hiring someone to automate your team’s work, this is the most honest sample I have: a real problem, a working system, and I use it every day.

        One scan, start to finish

        Every two hours during my working day it runs one scan. Most of it is plain Python; the agent does the reading and writing, and it can only use what I’ve written down about myself.

        1. Job postfrom a board, an alert mail or a company site
        2. Score0–100 against my rules; below 60 is logged and dropped
        3. Draftwritten for this role only, from facts about me
        4. Reviewa second agent checks every line
        5. Apply and mailit fills the application once I say yes, and mails the hiring address from the post
        6. Trackerone record per job, so nothing is sent twice

        What it does

        Finds the right posts

        It looks for automation and agent workflow roles on job boards, company pages and the alerts in my inbox, and drops anything outside what I do before it scores the rest.

        Writes one draft per role

        Posts that score 60 or more get a draft written for that company only. It works from a file of facts about me, and anything the file doesn’t back up is left blank for me to fill.

        Gets checked before it leaves

        A second agent reads every draft and fails it for a claim it can’t trace, a line that sounds like a template, or a company it didn’t actually name. Most drafts go round two or three times.

        Applies for me

        Once I say yes on Telegram, it fills the application on the company’s portal or Easy Apply, attaches my resume, and mails the hiring address from the post with the same tailored note.

        Handles the job portals

        Every scan it checks LinkedIn, Naukri, Indeed and the other boards for new posts, recruiter messages and profile views, and pulls anything that needs me to the top.

        Keeps my profiles current

        My resume, portfolio and portal profiles all say the same thing. When something goes stale it fixes it, and it refreshes Naukri every week so the profile doesn’t sink.

        Reads the news

        Once a day it writes me a short digest on agents, automation hiring and the tools I use. If something big lands, I hear about it the same day.

        Keeps me in the loop

        After every scan I get a short update on Telegram: mail, applications, new jobs, news. Replies and interview invites come with a button that opens the mail, and everything lands in one tracker, so nothing gets applied to twice.

        What it’s built with

        Nothing runs in the cloud. It lives in an LLM session on my laptop and stops when I close it at night.

        • Python
        • IMAP / SMTP
        • SQLite tracker
        • Telegram Bot API
        • Chrome for job boards
        • Written briefs and rules
        • Reviewer agent
        • Human sign-off
