Privacy

Last updated 4 October 2026

This site is a personal portfolio. It has no sign-up, no cookies and no advertising. This page explains what happens when you visit and when you use the contact form or the fit check.

What I collect

The contact form. Your name, email, the message, and the company, role and job link if you add them. They are saved in a small database on Cloudflare and sent to my phone through Telegram, so I can reply quickly. I use them only to reply.

The fit check. The job post is checked on the server and not kept. I save only its first 200 characters, so I can see which roles people check me for. The short summary at the top is written by Google Gemini from the list of matches only; the job post itself is never sent to Google.

The agent chat. Your questions are answered by Google Gemini using a short public summary of my work. I keep the first 300 characters of each question and answer so I can check the agent says the right things, and the first question of a chat is sent to my phone. If you ask the agent to set up a call, the name, email, company, role and times you give it are sent to me straight away, the same way as the contact form. Don’t put other personal details in the chat.

Spam protection. To limit how often one visitor can send, the server keeps a one-way hash of your IP address for up to 24 hours. The IP address itself is never stored.

Visitor counts. Cloudflare Web Analytics counts page views, the pages visited and the country they come from. It uses no cookies and doesn’t identify you.

Ask me to delete anything you sent and I will.

What third parties may see

Loading any web page means your browser asks some servers for files. These services receive your IP address and basic browser details as part of that request, under their own privacy policies:

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site. GitHub’s privacy statement.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces. Google’s privacy policy.
  • jsDelivr serves the 3D library used in the header. jsDelivr’s privacy policy.
  • Cloudflare runs the form, the agent chat, the fit check and the visitor counts. Cloudflare’s privacy policy.
  • Google Gemini answers the agent chat and writes the fit-check summary. Google’s privacy policy.
  • Telegram carries contact messages to my phone. Telegram’s privacy policy.

Storage in your browser

The site stores two small things in your browser: a flag in session storage so the start-up animation plays only once per visit, and your light or dark theme choice if you use the switch. Neither is a cookie, and neither is sent anywhere.

If you write to me

If you write to me by email or through the form, I use your email address and message only to reply and to discuss the work you asked about. I don’t add you to any list, and I don’t share your details with anyone. Ask me to delete our conversation at any time and I will.

Screenshots on this site

Every screenshot of a working tool uses sample or anonymised data. Real client, bank and borrower names are replaced or blurred.
