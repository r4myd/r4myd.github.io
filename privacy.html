Privacy

Last updated 25 September 2026

This site is a personal portfolio. It has no sign-up, no forms, no cookies, no analytics and no advertising. This page explains the little that does happen when you visit.

What I collect

Nothing, directly. I don’t run tracking scripts and I can’t see who visits.

What third parties may see

Loading any web page means your browser asks some servers for files. These services receive your IP address and basic browser details as part of that request, under their own privacy policies:

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site. GitHub’s privacy statement.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces. Google’s privacy policy.
  • jsDelivr serves the 3D library used in the header. jsDelivr’s privacy policy.

Storage in your browser

The site stores one small flag in your browser’s session storage so the start-up animation plays only once per visit. It is not a cookie, is never sent anywhere, and is cleared when you close the tab.

If you email me

If you write to me, I use your email address and message only to reply and to discuss the work you asked about. I don’t add you to any list, and I don’t share your details with anyone. Ask me to delete our conversation at any time and I will.

Screenshots on this site

Every screenshot of a working tool uses sample or anonymised data. Real client, bank and borrower names are replaced or blurred.
